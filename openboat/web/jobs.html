Counted, not judged — each line says what is missing. This project ships no intervals of its own, because an invented one gets followed.

Nothing is being counted yet

The service schedule is your engine's, not this software's — it ships no intervals, because "every 100 hours" is right for some engines and wrong for others, and a schedule that is confidently wrong gets followed. Take them from your own manual, in boat.toml:

[maintenance.impeller]
description = "Raw-water pump impeller"
hours = 100
months = 12

What is broken

Every entry is unverified: it is what somebody saw from a phone at the moment of noticing, confirmed by nobody since. Nothing here promotes that to a fact.

No faults on the list

Faults are filed from a phone, standing where the fault is — its own small service, on its own port, because this dashboard does not write to the boat. Start it on the machine the boat's files live on and open it on any phone on the same network:

python3 -m openboat.snag
